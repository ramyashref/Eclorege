Need to know

Good to know.

Answers to common questions about ordering, payment, and delivery.

Which payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery (COD) is the currently listed payment method.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is EGP 100 within Greater Cairo and EGP 150 outside Greater Cairo. Orders with a product subtotal of EGP 5,000 or more qualify for free shipping.

Can I review my order before confirming?

The checkout displays your selected products and the estimated total including shipping before you submit your order.

How do I choose the right product?

Please review the official product label and instructions. Confirm ingredients, intended use, and professional-use requirements with the manufacturer or a qualified professional where applicable.

When will my order be confirmed?

After the store’s order-processing system is connected, your order details can be received by the store team for confirmation and delivery coordination.
