Frequently asked questions

Helpful information about ordering, delivery, and product details.

How can I place an order?

Open the product page and select “Order on WhatsApp”. Your message will include the product name and price. Our team can confirm availability and delivery details.

What payment methods are available?

Payment and order confirmation are arranged directly with our team on WhatsApp. Please confirm the available method when placing your order.

How much is shipping?

Greater Cairo: EGP 100. Outside Greater Cairo: EGP 150. Complimentary delivery applies to orders of EGP 5,000 or more, subject to confirmation by our team.

Can I ask about a product before ordering?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp at 01080080203 and mention the product you are interested in.

Where can I find ingredients and instructions?

Please refer to the official product packaging and supplier documentation, or ask our team for verified product information.
