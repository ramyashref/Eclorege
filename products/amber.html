Amber

Skin Revitalization & Radiance

Proposed ingredient profile

  • Hydration: non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid / sodium hyaluronate.
  • Amino-acid complex: glycine, L-proline, L-lysine, and L-arginine.
  • Vitamin candidates: niacinamide (B3), panthenol (B5), pyridoxine (B6), and a suitable vitamin C derivative.
  • Antioxidant candidates: glutathione, subject to formulation validation.
  • Trace-element candidates: zinc and copper compounds.
  • Amber-associated candidate: succinic acid, subject to compatibility and safety testing.

Intended benefits

  • Skin hydration support
  • A refreshed, radiant-looking complexion
  • Smoother-looking skin
  • Support for the appearance of skin elasticity

Directions & professional use

Use only in accordance with the final manufacturer-approved instructions for the specific product and route of application. Injectable ingredients and combinations require appropriate pharmaceutical, clinical, sterility, and regulatory validation.

Product information
